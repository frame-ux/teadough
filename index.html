OUR STORY

Doughnuts & Tea
since 1803

Founded by brother and sister Arthur and Hannah, Teadough began as a university venture inspired by a family recipe for “dow nuts” dating back to 1803.

We traded at the Edinburgh Fringe, turning over £10,000 in our first month, before developing a doughnut-making kit.
